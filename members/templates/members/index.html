Getting started

Enter your M-Pesa number to receive a secure STK Push. Confirm the one-time KES 130 payment on your phone.

Membership is for adults aged 18 and older. The KES 130 fee does not renew automatically.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is HeartConnect a dating site?+

No. HeartConnect is for friendly conversation, cultural exchange, and companionship. It doesn’t offer dating or matchmaking.

What does the KES 130 payment cover?+

It is a one-time community membership payment. There is no automatic renewal. Your access starts after payment and team confirmation.

How long does confirmation take?+

Membership is reviewed by our team after payment. We’ll email you when your account is confirmed. If you’re waiting, check your inbox and spam folder.

Who can join?+

Adults aged 18 and older who want respectful, friendly conversations with people in other countries.

Can I stop someone from contacting me?+

Yes. You can block a member from a conversation, or report the conversation for team review.
